# Live mAIcro.app design notes (21 Nov 2026)

Captured from browser + CSS (`index-G3FdcLkM.css`).

## Colours
- Near-white bg ~#FBFDFE; pale ice blue band ~#F4F9FD
- Deep navy footer / headings ~#102b40 / #12293F
- Charcoal logo panel ~#1e1e1e
- Accent steel blue ~#2877a8 / #2F7DB0 (hero keyword highlight)
- Sky blue ~#4fa6e5 / #3FA0E0 (AI in logotype, asterisk)
- Card tint ~#DCEBF6 / #c9e2ef
- Body slate ~#4b6273 / #536a7b
- Soft borders #102b4026

## Type
- system-ui / humanist sans (Noto/Open Sans feel)
- Headlines extra-bold, tight tracking
- Eyebrow labels: tiny, bold, uppercase, wide tracking (e.g. "A PLACE TO BEGIN")
- Brand: mAIcro with AI in blue

## Layout
- Airy single narrow centred column ~800px, left-aligned content
- Generous vertical padding, hairline section dividers
- Hero two-column: copy left, dark logo panel right
- Feature blocks: card left / text right
- Closing CTA: heading left, button right

## Components
- Primary button: solid navy, white bold text, arrow ↗, ~6px radius
- Secondary: underlined blue text links ending →
- Cards: large radius 14–16px, light blue tint, soft border, no heavy shadow
- Interest picker: white rounded container, pale-blue tiles with icons
- Logo panel: white wordmark, blue AI, connected-nodes glyph for o
- Feature graphic: large blue eight-spoke asterisk ("A LITTLE SPARK")
- Footer navy: wordmark + tagline left; links right; hairline + "Made for curious minds..."

## Tone
Warm, encouraging: "Good things start small", "Let's make room for your ideas"
